## 4. Results

Send these by WhatsApp. If you also send them by email from ro.bio.muse@gmail.com, send them in small batches.

### Selected as a core member

```
Hi [Name], congratulations! 🎉
You have been selected as a *core member* of RO-BIO in the *[Squad]* squad.

Induction: [day], [time], [place].
Please join your squad group: [WhatsApp group link]

Your first big job: Bot Forge on 18 Nov. See you there!
RO-BIO
```

### Invited to work with a squad (internal name: supporting member)

Do not use the words "supporting member" in the message itself.

```
Hi [Name], thank you for coming to the RO-BIO interview. We really liked your interest in [Squad].

We would love you to work with the *[Squad]* squad on Bot Forge (18 Nov) and our Saturday sessions. Would you like to join?
Reply YES and we will add you to the group.

RO-BIO
```

### Not selected this time

```
Hi [Name], thank you for applying to RO-BIO and for coming to the interview.

We had many strong applicants and only a few seats, so we could not select you this time. Please do not let that stop you: come to Bot Forge on 18 Nov and to our Saturday sessions, and apply again next time.

Follow @ro_bio_hub for what's next.
RO-BIO
```
